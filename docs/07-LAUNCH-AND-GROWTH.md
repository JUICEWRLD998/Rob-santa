## 2. Maturity & Compliance questionnaire (do it early)

**Why it matters:** an experience without content-maturity information is treated like a "Moderate" one. It isn't recommended to younger players, the biggest group on Roblox.

**Requirements:** the owning account must be **at least 30 days old**. Yours joined 2026-11-02, so you're eligible from **Wed 2026-12-02**. Do it that day. If the label still isn't showing on Sun Dec 6, contact Roblox Support. Don't wait until launch day to find out.

**How:**
1. Go to **Creator Hub → Creations**, open the experience.
2. Find **Maturity & Compliance** (under the Audience section; menu names move, so search the page for "Maturity" if needed).
3. Answer **honestly** for what's in the game: cartoon caper, snowballs that stun, no blood, no weapons that hurt, standard filtered chat. **Lucky Wrap counts as a paid random item** (you pay Robux to change a random roll), so say yes if asked about paid random items. The game shows the odds before purchase and hides Lucky Wrap where Roblox's policy restricts it (built in Phase 6).
4. Expected result: **Minimal** or **Mild**. If you get something higher, read which answer caused it before changing anything in the game.
5. Roblox reviews answers and can act on wrong ones. Never shade the truth to get a lower label.

Answers can be updated after each update (e.g. if you add something new).

## 4. Icon and thumbnails

The icon and thumbnail decide whether anyone clicks. They matter more than any single feature.

### Icon (square)
- One subject, huge: an elf's shocked face next to a gift box with eyes peeking out. Bright, warm colours on a dark blue background.
- Must be readable at thumbnail size on a phone. Squint test: still clear when tiny and blurry?
- No small text.

### Thumbnails (16:9; upload several)
1. **The signature moment:** an elf sniffing a gift box in a glowing workshop, the player's eyes peeking out. Big text: **DON'T MOVE!**
2. **The loot:** a Golden Mythic toy bursting out of a present, coins flying.
3. **The chase:** a player running with a big present, three elves behind, "!" over their heads.

How to make them: Claude poses the scene in Studio and captures it (MCP screenshot, with the camera set up). Then add text and touch-ups in any image editor. Don't make them look like real Roblox UI or official Roblox art.

### Test them
Make 2–3 icon versions. Ad campaigns (section 6) show which one people click most. Keep the winner.

## 6. Getting the first players: ads

What your own scouting data shows (Thief Simulator, Oct–Nov 2026): **Sponsored Ads** brought the first players from Oct 21. Once players kept coming back, **Home Recommendation** (the algorithm) took over around Nov 2 and became the biggest source.

The plan:
1. **Roblox Ads Manager** (ads.roblox.com) → create a **Sponsored Experience** campaign.
2. Start small, with a daily budget you're comfortable losing completely, for 3–5 days. Check current pricing and payment options in Ads Manager.
3. Watch **new users by source** and **D1 retention** in analytics, not just clicks.
4. If D1 retention is decent (aim for 12%+), keep spending and raise it slowly. If it's bad, stop spending, fix the first 3 minutes, try again. Ads can't save a game players leave.

Also free:
- **Friends and family** play on launch day (likes and the first sessions help).
- Your **Roblox Community**: members get notified of updates.

## 7. Clips (TikTok / YouTube Shorts)

Rob Santa was designed to make clips. Post 1–3 short videos a week:
- The disguise: an elf sniffs, and you *almost* move. Text: "the elf has no idea 😭"
- A Golden Mythic unwrap reaction.
- A 3-elf chase that ends in a disguise save.

Record in-game (Roblox has a built-in recorder, or use any screen recorder), vertical crop, under 20 seconds, hook in the first second. Put the game name on screen.

## 8. Numbers to watch (Creator Hub → Analytics)

| Number | What it tells you | Benchmark / target |
|---|---|---|
| **D1 retention** | % of new players back the next day. The algorithm cares about this most | Your scouted thief sim: 8.7%. **Target: 12%+** |
| D7 retention | Long-term stickiness | Target: 3%+ |
| Average session time | How long a visit lasts | Thief Simulator: 4.4 min. Target: 10+ min |
| Average playtime | Total time per user per day | Thief sim: 19.8 min |
| New users by source | Where players come from (ads, recommendations, search, friends) | Watch Home Recommendation grow |
| Tutorial funnel | Where new players quit in the first 3 minutes (Phase 6 events) | Fix the step with the biggest drop first |
| Robux per day / paying users | Money | Thief sim: about 3,300 R$/day at ~327 players online |
| Errors (error report) | Bugs players hit | Zero new errors after each update |

The weekly question: **"What's the biggest drop, and what's one change that would fix it?"**

## 10. Money: how Robux turns into cash

- When a player buys a game pass or developer product, **you get about 70%** of the price in Robux. Roblox keeps the rest.
- New earnings show as **pending** for a few days before you can use them.
- **DevEx** (Developer Exchange) turns earned Robux into real money:
  - Standard rate: **$0.0038 per earned Robux** (Roblox docs).
  - Minimum to cash out: **30,000 earned Robux** (= $114 at the standard rate).
  - Robux earned before Sept 5, 2025 convert at the older $0.0035 rate. That doesn't apply to you.
  - Since April 2026, Roblox pays a **higher rate (+42%)** on Robux spent by age-verified US players aged 18+ in qualifying games. Rob Santa targets kids, so expect mostly the standard rate.
  - Requirements: you must be 13+, have a verified email, meet the Developer Exchange terms, and have a way to receive payment. Check the current requirements on the DevEx page before you apply.
- Example: your scouted thief sim makes about 3,300 Robux a day. At 70% that's about 2,300 earned Robux a day, roughly $8.80/day or $260/month at the standard rate. This shows the scale of a 300-player game.

**Rule:** reinvest the first earnings into ads only if D1 retention is healthy (section 6). Otherwise put them into fixing the first 3 minutes.
